Prime Produce Limited provides operational, strategic, and creative support services for not-for-profit leaders and programs. We represent a grassroots ecosystem of friends, families, and neighbors committed to supporting community service initiatives.

From special events to service design to community engagement, our community has partnered with many mission-aligned organizations who need a fresh perspective or an extra pair of hands to realize their vision. Time and talent can go a long way.

We also orchestrate service grants, broker discounted rates, and provide recommendations to great vendors.

If you’d like to partner up and/or inquire about hiring, drop us a line with what you’re looking for: **prevenience@primeproduce.org**
